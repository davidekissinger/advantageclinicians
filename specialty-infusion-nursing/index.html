Potential focus areas

Specialty programs demand clinical judgment.

Partner discussions may involve immunoglobulin therapies, specialty biologics, antimicrobials, or infusion-related assessment and education. Mentioning a category does not mean a service is currently available or approved.

Therapy-specific support would depend on clinician competence, orders and protocols, contracting, legal authority, and an accountable care-delivery organization.

Frequently asked

Clarity before the first conversation.

Can a pharmacy request a specific patient visit?

No. This site collects general business inquiries only; it is not a referral portal and cannot accept patient information or medical orders.

Is nursing coverage available immediately?

Availability is not represented or guaranteed. This site supports exploratory staffing discussions, subject to qualified personnel and approved operations.

Does RN interest confirm an assignment or employment?

No. Interest initiates screening conversations only. Eligibility requires separate verification, competency, and contractual review.

Where will this network operate?

The initial focus is home-based infusion nursing within Ohio. County-level staffing capacity is not established, and no visits or coverage are guaranteed.

Business and clinician inquiries only. Do not submit patient names, diagnoses, orders, medical records, or other protected health information. This site does not accept referrals or provide emergency care.
