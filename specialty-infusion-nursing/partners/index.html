For program leaders

Operational clarity matters as much as coverage.

The website is a starting point for a potential commercial relationship, not a patient scheduling application.

Before any actual engagement, parties must establish who employs or contracts with the nurse, who directs clinical care, how qualifications are verified, and how orders and documentation are securely handled.

Possible discussion topics
  • Home, ambulatory or facility settings
  • General monthly demand bands
  • Territories and travel considerations
  • Training and competency expectations
  • Contracting and care-team responsibilities
