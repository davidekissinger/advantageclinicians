Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Advantage Clinicians handles information collected through AdvantageClinicians.com (the “Site”).

Information we collect

When you contact us through the Site, we may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, organization, the type of inquiry, and the contents of your message.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the Site, such as IP address, browser information, device information, request logs, and timestamps.

How we use information

  • To respond to staffing, clinician, and general inquiries.
  • To communicate with you about your request.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of others.

Healthcare information

The Site is intended for business and recruiting inquiries. Please do not use the contact form to submit patient medical records, protected health information, or information needed for urgent patient care. The Site is not a patient-care portal.

Service providers

We may use service providers to host the Site, process website form submissions, provide technical services, and support Site operations. Those providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to perform those services.

Cookies and third-party resources

The current Site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. The Site may request web fonts from a third-party font provider, which may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details when those font files are delivered.

Sharing and sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information collected through the Site. We may disclose information to service providers acting on our behalf, when required by law, to protect safety or rights, or as part of a business transaction involving Advantage Clinicians.

Data retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, maintain appropriate business records, and meet legal or operational requirements.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information submitted through the Site. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us through the Site to ask about information you previously submitted or to request that we update or delete it, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for healthcare organizations and adults exploring professional opportunities. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site.

Links to other websites

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. Advantage Clinicians is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our Site or practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through our Contact Us page.

This policy describes the current website implementation and may need to be updated if analytics, applicant tracking, job-board, advertising, or other data-processing features are added.
